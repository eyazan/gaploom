# Competitor Analysis (preliminary)

Access date for all URLs: **2026-11-22**. Research was done with a general web-search tool
from a cloud container. Store pages, SteamDB and review sites were seen only as search-result
summaries; no page was opened in a browser, no app was installed or played, and no paid market
intelligence tool (AppMagic, Sensor Tower, data.ai) was used.

Legend: **[V]** = verified from a cited source (as summarized by search results),
**[A]** = assumption / designer judgement, **[NR]** = not researched or nothing found.

## 3. Genre-level observations

* **Block / screw / sort puzzles** dominate hybrid-casual puzzle revenue. AppMagic's Q1 2025 top-10 split: block ≈71%, screw ≈20% of hybrid-casual puzzle revenue. [V] https://appmagic.rocks/blog/hybridcasual-q1-2025
* Common player complaints in free puzzle games: ad frequency, ads that crash or cost progress, suspected monetization-driven difficulty spikes, subscription "remove ads". [V, anecdotal aggregator data] https://www.techradar.com/pro/the-dark-side-of-free-mobile-games-how-developers-reverse-engineer-your-patience-to-milk-ad-revenue , https://unstar.app/blog/candy-crush-royal-match-gardenscapes-homescapes-toon-blast-puzzle-games-ranked-2026
* Premium narrative/art puzzle games are reviewed as short (Gorogoa, Donut County). [V] see links above.

## 4. Implications for GAPLOOM [A]

1. "Hole"/"void"/"negative space" vocabulary is crowded. Lead marketing with the *verb* ("move the gap, the world rewires") and the *restoration payoff*, not with "negative space".
2. The light family alone reads as "yet another laser puzzle" in a screenshot. Every store screenshot must show a gap **in flight** and the source turning solid.
3. Prop Swap feedback → transferable items must be visually unmistakable (done: glowing tokens, sockets outlined in slab colour, pulsing force hot-spots).
4. Monetization: avoid the complaint profile of ad-heavy hybrid-casual; premium unlock fits the cozy/premium audience better.
