## English
**Name:** GAPLOOM *(provisional — trademark check pending)*
**Subtitle (30):** Move the gaps, light the world
**Promotional text (170):** A quiet puzzle about empty space. Move glowing gaps between clay slabs, guide light and little carts, and bring five miniature worlds back to life.

**Description**
Nothing changes everything.

In GAPLOOM you never move blocks — you move the *gaps* in them. A glass gap lets light through. A groove lets a little cart roll by. Coloured glass paints the light. Every gap you take leaves solid stone behind, and a gap can only land where its light or cart is pressing.

• 29 handcrafted puzzles in five worlds — a lighthouse, a railway, a greenhouse, a clock tower and a glassworks
• A new daily puzzle every day, with streaks and a spoiler-free share card
• No timers, no lives, no ads: unlimited undo, restart and two-step hints
• Relaxing original music and hand-made miniature dioramas that come back to life
• Colour-blind friendly (shapes, not only colours), offline, no account
